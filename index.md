### Promoting Global Awareness Around the World
        
  The aim of this website is to promote knowledge and awareness of current global problems, along with solutions that can be used in an attempt to make the world a better place. Though I feel that we already have many problems to deal with in our everyday lives without needing more, so that is why I decided to create this website, in order to have anyone, whoever they are, to make an impact and difference on the world we live in. Every month I will continue to write about a new world problem and how you can make a difference.
