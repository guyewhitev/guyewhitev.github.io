What makes a good country or a good government? Think about it. Why would you put your faith in your government? The answer lies in the trust and confidence in being represented and given a voice. 

Now, something bad is happening to our country and the world behind the scenes. From Europe to North America and Asia, all around the world something is changing the way the world governments are run and the choices that are made. Have you ever experienced peer pressure? Imagine it 10 times worse. To the point of threats. Anonymous senders have been threatening members of Congress in order to influence the government. 

Yes, threats are part of the problem now. Recently members of Congress and other government officials have reported notes, calls, and letters addressed to them bearing threats of murder, raping, and more to them and others around them. In extreme cases, threats to officials and their families have caused some governors and Congress members to be forced to change their public viewpoint that they are arguing for or against while they personally believe something else. In other cases, some officials have been threatened to the point of forcing them to add security measures and change daily life, and in the end, quit their job and move to somewhere else. 

This is called political divisiveness or polarization, sometimes referred to as the “Great Divide”. Again, political divisiveness is happening all around the world. Political divisiveness is essentially disagreeing on how our government should be run, but so extremely that it is impossible to discuss or even talk about the opposing side. Disagreeing about running a country or government is entirely normal and is in fact good for a government. Political divisiveness is now reaching the point where it is leading to polarized views, blaming, violence, riots, and threats. 



Social media is not helping either. Popular social media platforms such as X, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube are serving as places where opinionated stories can escalate. 

This makes it so that people such as members of Congress and other people are trying to use any means to prove they are right about their beliefs. They are trying to prove that they are doing the right thing to other people. In the constant chaos of self-righteousness about beliefs, it spirals out of control to the point of threats and violence.

So why is political divisiveness happening? I think we are trying to prove we are right about what we think and believe, so much that it is leading to harmful actions. Why do we care about our government? It’s because we care about the world we live in and we want to have a say in what happens in our world. What you should take away from this is that a good portion of the problem is misperception on what you see. When you look at something, try to see the larger picture and even though it is hard, try to put yourself in someone else’s point of view. Part of understanding the world we live in is first trying to understand the past. Without differences or disagreements, the world would not be the way it is today. Differences have built the world we live in today. 




## How you can make a difference

I think that the solution to political divisiveness is that we need to listen to what others say. In addition to that, we need to realize that even with the most politically polarized person, we still have something in common with them. In the end, the first step to beginning to accept and understand contradicting sides is to acknowledge that we have mutual beliefs with others.

With that being said, I think that there could be a better effort of trying to reduce polarization in everyday life. Small groups or leadership trainings are good examples. They create an open and safe space to share thoughts and realize that people with different beliefs are still regular people.

Just having a community group or a larger group to share your thoughts on political divisiveness, and doing activities or guided conversations can help people learn to work together with people that have opposed beliefs or opinions. This can help create a better understanding and environment in the world that will improve the general “mood” caused by extreme political divisiveness. 

#### So, you can

###### 1: Don't believe everything you read on the internet. 

###### 2: Keep an open mindset and listen to someone else's point of view.

###### 3: Remember that we all have things in common with each other.

###### 4: Occasionally take a break from social media if needed
